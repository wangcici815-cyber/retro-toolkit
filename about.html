About RetroToolkit

The most immersive free retro audio & VHS video toolkit on the internet — built for creators who believe imperfection is a feature.

What Is RetroToolkit?

RetroToolkit is a free, browser-based collection of retro audio and video tools for musicians, YouTubers, indie game developers, and anyone who loves the aesthetic of 70s–90s analog media.

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Your audio and images are never uploaded to a server — all processing happens locally on your device. That means zero privacy risk, zero cost, and zero waiting.

Alongside the tools, we publish in-depth articles on the history and science behind retro audio and video technology — because understanding why tape sounds warm, or why VHS looks the way it does, makes you a better creator.

Our Philosophy
🔒
Privacy First

We built every tool to run locally in your browser. We have no idea what files you process, and we never will. That's intentional.

💸
Free Forever

All tools are free with no "premium" tier, no watermark, no time limit. The site is sustained by non-intrusive Google AdSense ads.

📚
Content + Tools Together

Every tool is paired with a deep-dive article explaining the history and science behind the effect. We believe in teaching, not just clicking.

🎨
Aesthetics Matter

We built the site to feel like the era it represents — a CRT terminal in an 80s studio. The medium is part of the message.
